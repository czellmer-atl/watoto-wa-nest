Thank you for your donation!

Your help goes straight to the children at The Nest in Limuru – for food, school, medical care and a safe home.

What happens next?

Donation receipt: If you shared your address with us on PayPal, we will send you the receipt. Otherwise, drop us a line with your name and postal address.

Stay in touch: We share life at The Nest on Instagram. How your donation is used is set out in our annual report.

Spread the word: Tell friends and family about The Nest – every further donation helps.
