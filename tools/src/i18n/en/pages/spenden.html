Gift certificates

Give a little differently than usual.

Again and again we want to make someone happy on a special occasion. Yet somehow everyone already has everything they need. Still want to give a surprise? Then we have an idea for you.

Donate an amount of your choice and receive a gift certificate for the person you want to surprise.

  1. Choose an amount

    €10 school supplies · €40 food · €60 baby food · €300 monthly salary of a house mother · €600 monthly salary of a nurse – each for one month.

  2. Transfer

    Transfer the amount to our donation account and put the code “Geschenk-Urkunde” and your email address in the payment reference, e.g. Geschenk-Urkunde; jane.doe[at]email.com.

  3. Receive your certificate

    We email you your personal gift certificate. Print it in colour and wrap it however you like. The recipient can see exactly what was achieved in their name.
